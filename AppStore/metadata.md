## Version 1.0

**Promotional text**

Copy any picture onto paper. Make it see-through over the camera, fit it to the page, and trace!

**Description**

Trace4Kids turns your iPhone or iPad into a tracing helper for children aged 4 and up.

Pick a picture from your photos and it appears over the camera's view of your paper. Slide See-through until you can see both, then drag and pinch the picture to fit the page. Prop the device above your paper, look at the screen and draw over the lines.

• See-through slider to blend the picture with your paper
• Drag, pinch and turn the picture to fit any page
• Lock it in place so little hands can't move it by accident
• Pink corner marks to dot on your paper, so you can line the picture up again another day
• Your picture and its position are saved automatically
• Lines mode shows just the outlines, which are easier to follow
• Light box mode for tracing with thin paper laid on the screen
• Big, friendly buttons that young children can use
• Works on iPhone and iPad

Safe for children: no ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases, no links out of the app and no internet connection. Pictures come only from the photos already on your device, and the camera view is never recorded or saved.

## App Review notes

Trace4Kids overlays a picture chosen with the system photo picker on the live back camera, so the user can trace it onto paper. The camera feed is only displayed, never recorded. No account or login is needed. To test without a stand, pick any photo and use the See-through slider; Light box mode turns the screen white instead of using the camera.
