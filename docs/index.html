Trace4Kids helps children aged 4 and up copy any picture onto paper. Pick a picture from your photos, make it see-through over the camera, fit it to your paper, and trace what you see on the screen. Mark the pink corners on your paper and you can carry on another day, with the picture lined up exactly where it was.

What it does

  • Opens pictures from the photo library on your iPhone or iPad. Nothing is downloaded from the internet.
  • See-through slider to blend the picture with the live camera view of your paper.
  • Drag, pinch and turn to fit the picture to the page, then lock it in place.
  • Pink corner marks to draw on your paper, so you can line the picture up again later.
  • Lines mode shows just the outlines, which are easier to follow.
  • Light box mode turns the screen white for tracing with thin paper laid on top.

No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases, no links out of the app, and no data collected.
