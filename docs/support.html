How to trace

  1. Tap Pick a picture and choose one from your photos.
  2. Prop your iPhone or iPad over your paper, about a ruler's height above it, with the back camera looking down. A phone stand, tablet stand or a pile of books works.
  3. Slide See-through until you can see your paper and the picture together. Drag to move the picture, pinch to change its size.
  4. Draw a small dot on your paper under each pink corner, then tap Lock.
  5. Look at the screen and draw over the lines.

Carrying on later

Your picture and where it was are saved. Tap Keep tracing, then move the picture until the pink corners sit on your dots, and tap Lock again.

Common questions

The camera is black. Turn on camera access in Settings → Trace4Kids → Camera.

The picture keeps turning when the device is flat. Turn on Rotation Lock in Control Centre.

I can't see enough of my paper. On devices with an ultra-wide camera, tap See more, or raise the device a little higher.
