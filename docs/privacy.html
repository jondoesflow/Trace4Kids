Privacy

HomeSupportPrivacy

Trace4Kids collects no data at all.

Photos

You choose a picture with the system photo picker. Trace4Kids only sees the one picture you pick, and needs no permission to your photo library. A copy of that picture is kept inside the app on your device so you can carry on tracing later. Picking a new picture replaces it.

Camera

The camera shows your paper on the screen while you trace. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

No network, no tracking

The app makes no internet connections. It has no analytics, advertising, accounts, third-party code or in-app purchases. Nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

Children

Trace4Kids is made for children aged 4 and up. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the saved picture and its settings.
